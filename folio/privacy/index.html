Folio Privacy Policy

Kuwanasa Interactive · Effective 7 October 2026 · Applies to Folio for Android (com.kuwanasa.folio)

Folio is a PDF reader, scanner and toolkit. It was built to keep your documents on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles, what third parties receive, and the choices you have.

Your documents stay on your device

PDFs you open, scan, edit, merge, split, compress, convert or protect are processed entirely on your device. Kuwanasa Interactive does not run servers for Folio and never receives, uploads or stores your documents, their contents, your annotations or your reading history. Text recognition (OCR) uses Google ML Kit's on-device model, so scanned images are not sent anywhere for recognition.

Folio keeps a local library database (file names, page counts, reading position, favourites, tags, folders and extracted text) and your settings in the app's private storage. That data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Camera: only for scanning documents when you choose to. Images stay on your device.
  • Notifications: only for the optional reading reminder you can switch on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally.
  • Biometrics: only for the optional app lock. Fingerprint and face data are handled by Android; Folio never sees them.
  • Internet: used only by the Google advertising and Google Play Billing services described below. Folio's own features work offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Folio shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the browsing screens and an occasional full-screen ad after you finish reading a document. Ads never appear while a document is open. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and general (non-precise) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps) and diagnostic data.

Folio does not send Google your documents or anything you read. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and certain US states), Folio uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Ad Preferences. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). Subscribing to Folio Pro removes ads completely, and the app stops requesting them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Folio Pro is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Google processes the payment; Kuwanasa Interactive never receives your card or payment details. The app receives only the purchase status from Google Play so it can unlock Pro features, and it stores that status on your device. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in is required, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs other than the advertising and billing services above.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share your documents with anyone.

Sharing files

When you use Share or Save to send a document to another app or location, it goes only to the destination you choose, and that destination's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Folio stores lives on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. Advertising data held by Google is subject to Google's retention policies and controls.

Security

Folio keeps its data in Android's app-private storage. The optional app lock and the AES-256 password protection for PDFs add further protection. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we never transmit your documents.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. Because Folio does not send us your data, most requests can be met directly on your device as described above. For advertising data, use the consent controls in the app or contact Google. You can also contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
